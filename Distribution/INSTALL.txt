Bar Keeper's Friend

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe). Includes Apple silicon and Intel binaries.

1. Quit any running copy of Bar Keeper's Friend.
2. Drag BarKeepersFriend.app to Applications.
3. Open Bar Keeper's Friend from Applications, then eject this disk image.

The app lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon. Right-click its icon for
Settings. In Settings > Items, drag icons between bars or reorder them, then
choose Apply Changes. Option-Command-B reveals hidden items.

Basic in-place hiding needs no permissions. Settings > General explains the
optional Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. macOS may require
re-approval when replacing an earlier source-signed build.
